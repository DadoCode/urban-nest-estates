The house

Five bedrooms, a private garden and the Elizabeth line close by

Designed for groups who want the space and privacy of an entire home, the property has five generous double bedrooms, an open-plan living area, a fully equipped kitchen and a private garden. There is plenty of room to spend time together without feeling on top of one another, making it particularly well suited to families, groups and project teams.

The Elizabeth line is just a short walk away, with direct connections to Liverpool Street, Bond Street and Heathrow. Stratford and the Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park are also nearby.
