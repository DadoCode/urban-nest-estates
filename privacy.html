Your information

Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information Urban Nest Estates Ltd collects through this website, why we collect it, who it is shared with and what rights you have over it. It was last updated in August 2026.

Who we are

Urban Nest Estates Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales, company number 15818476. We operate serviced accommodation and provide property management services in London. For the purposes of UK data protection law we are the data controller for the information described here.

You can reach us at info@urbannestestates.co.uk or on +44 7832 827 872.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us. When you submit an enquiry through this website, that is your name, your email address, and where you provide them your telephone number, the dates you are interested in, the number of guests, and anything you write in the message field. If you enquire from a particular property page we also record which property you were viewing, so that we can answer you properly.

We do not ask for payment details through this website, and we do not run advertising or profiling cookies on it.

Why we use it

We use the information you send to respond to your enquiry, to answer questions about availability and rates, to arrange a stay or a management agreement, and to keep a record of our correspondence with you. Our lawful basis is that processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact our business.

We will not add you to a marketing list on the strength of an enquiry. If we ever send you marketing, it will be because you asked us to, and every message will let you unsubscribe.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for as long as we may reasonably need them, which is normally up to two years from our last contact with you, so that we can pick up a conversation where it left off. Where an enquiry becomes a booking or an agreement, we keep the related records for as long as tax and accounting rules require. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Cookies

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may set a strictly necessary cookie to protect the site from automated abuse. If we add visitor analytics in future, we will use a privacy-preserving service that does not identify individuals, and we will update this policy.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, to have it corrected if it is wrong, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. To exercise any of these, email info@urbannestestates.co.uk and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information we will update this page and change the date at the top.
