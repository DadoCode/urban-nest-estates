What we do

Our services

Property Management

We manage every aspect of your home, from listing and pricing to guest care, housekeeping and maintenance. You retain the income it generates and receive clear monthly reporting, so you always know how your property is performing.

Serviced Apartments

Beautifully furnished homes prepared with everything needed for an effortless stay, including hotel-quality linen, a fully equipped kitchen, fast Wi-Fi and a dedicated workspace. Thoughtful details, from toiletries and hairdryers to a guest journal for capturing favourite moments, make every stay feel considered and personal. Available by the night, month or season.

Rent Guarantee

Receive an agreed fixed payment on the same date each month for the full term. We take complete responsibility for the day-to-day running of your property, giving you reliable income without the ongoing management.

For property owners

Your property, expertly managed

We manage every detail, from professional photography and dynamic pricing to guest screening, housekeeping, maintenance and round-the-clock support. Choose full management with transparent monthly reporting, or a fixed monthly rent while we take care of the rest.
