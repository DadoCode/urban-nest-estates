The apartment

Lakeside, with King's Cross 15 minutes away

A peaceful lakeside apartment to come home to. Mornings start with coffee on the balcony and a walk beside the Welsh Harp Reservoir, and King's Cross is fifteen minutes away by direct train. Guests return to this one for the calm, and for how easy it is to reach the centre.

The apartment is a modern 48 square metre fourth floor flat with a double bedroom, a versatile living area with a sofa bed, a fully equipped kitchen and a private balcony. Guests also have complimentary gym access and use of the shared terrace garden.
