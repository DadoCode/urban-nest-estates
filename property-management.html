The approach

Hospitality, brought to your property

We run every home on a hospitality model, pairing the comfort of a private apartment with hotel grade service. You keep one accountable point of contact, and a team that treats the property as if it were their own.

Whether you want full management on a revenue share, or a completely hands off arrangement with guaranteed monthly rent, we tailor the setup around your property and how involved you want to be.

Working with us

Two ways to work with us

Both options offer the same attentive care and professional management. You simply choose between the earning potential of full management and the reassurance of a fixed monthly payment.

01

Full Management

We analyse demand for the property's location, set responsive pricing, manage bookings and guest care, coordinate housekeeping, and oversee ongoing maintenance.

You receive the income your property generates, less our management fee, alongside a clear monthly report covering occupancy, average nightly rate and revenue. Stronger performance means greater earning potential for you.

02

Rent Guarantee

You receive a fixed, pre-agreed payment on the same date each month for the duration of the term. Our team manages the marketing, operating costs and day-to-day care of the property.

You enjoy predictable income, a professionally maintained home and the reassurance of having nothing further to manage.

The process

From onboarding to five-star stays

01

Onboarding & Listing

We begin by styling and photographing the property, then create a refined listing description that captures its character, key features and surrounding neighbourhood. Once complete, the home is launched across leading booking platforms.

02

Pricing & Distribution

We monitor demand throughout the year and adjust pricing in response to market conditions, seasonality and local activity. This keeps the property competitively positioned during both peak periods and quieter months.

03

Guest Experience

Every booking is carefully reviewed and identity-verified before arrival. Our team provides attentive support, responds promptly to requests and ensures each stay runs seamlessly.

04

Housekeeping & Care

Between every stay, the home is professionally cleaned, fresh linen and towels are provided, and essentials are replenished. Ongoing maintenance is handled promptly, keeping the property beautifully presented and ready to welcome every guest.

How we position a property

Positioned for the right guest

Every home has its own character, location and ideal guest.

We begin by understanding who the property is genuinely best suited to, then shape every detail around that audience, from the photography and listing description to the minimum stay and pricing strategy. By positioning each home with clarity and purpose, we attract the right guests, support stronger occupancy and maximise its earning potential.
