What's next for us

Growing our collection with purpose

Urban Nest Estates is expanding across London, with a particular focus on Zones 1 and 2. We welcome opportunities ranging from individual homes of any size to entire residential blocks of up to thirty apartments, including properties available for outright purchase.

What we're looking for

Property across London zones one and two

We are looking for units across London zones one and two, of any size, as well as full blocks of up to thirty apartments. We are also open to purchasing blocks outright.

If you own or represent an individual property, portfolio or residential block that could complement our growing collection, we would be pleased to hear from you.

Whether you are an owner thinking about selling, an agent with the right stock, or simply someone who knows of an opportunity, we would like to talk. We move quickly and deal straightforwardly.

Our criteria

What suits us

Location

London zones one and two, in the well connected neighbourhoods our guests already look for.

Any size

Single units through to full blocks of up to thirty apartments. Studios, one and two beds, HMOs and more.

Whole blocks

We are open to purchasing blocks outright, as well as taking them on to manage.

For companies and relocation agents

Housing a team, or a family arriving in London

If you are placing employees in London, or you are a relocation agent looking for the right home for a client, we would like to hear from you. The collection runs from one-bedroom apartments in Chelsea, Kensington and Marylebone through to a five-bedroom house with a private garden, so we can house one person on a project as comfortably as a family or a team arriving together.

Tell us the dates, the area you need and how many people you are housing, and we will show you what is available. If nothing in the collection is the right fit, we will talk through what else we can arrange.

Tell us what you need See the collection
  • Whole apartments and houses, not hotel rooms
  • Homes across Chelsea, Kensington, the West End, Marylebone, Limehouse, Hendon and Forest Gate
  • One bedroom through to five, sleeping four to eight guests
  • Stays of a few nights, several months or a full relocation
  • Reduced rates on longer bookings
  • Fully equipped kitchens, laundry and fresh hotel-quality linen
  • Professional cleaning before every stay
  • One point of contact from first enquiry through to check-out
