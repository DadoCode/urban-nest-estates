The apartment

Steps from Oxford Street and Soho

A design-led three-bedroom residence in the heart of the West End, moments from Tottenham Court Road, Soho and Covent Garden. Sleeping up to six guests, the apartment pairs elegant interiors with marble bathrooms, generous living spaces and a dining area designed for gathering.

The principal suite features a king-size bed dressed in 400-thread-count linen, complemented by two further double bedrooms with hotel-quality bedding and blackout blinds. A lift provides direct access to the floor, while portable air conditioning in every bedroom ensures a comfortable stay throughout the warmer months.

Living & dining

Open plan · seats six

An open reception room anchored by a plush velvet sofa, a copper leaf media wall and a bioethanol fireplace. The dining table seats six comfortably, and a selection of board games and curated London artwork give the room a lived-in warmth. High ceilings and chevron oak throughout.

Principal suite

King · 400 thread count

A restful principal bedroom with a king bed dressed in 400 thread count linen, a feature fireplace and panelled walls beneath an Art Deco chandelier. Blackout blinds, bespoke wardrobes and considered bedside lighting.

Further bedrooms

Two doubles

Two further double bedrooms with hotel grade bedding, blackout blinds and generous storage. The third has its own television and a dressing nook. Portable air conditioning is available for all three rooms.

Bathrooms

Marble · brushed gold

Two bathrooms finished in marble with brushed gold fittings and illuminated mirrors, stocked with Odyssey toiletries. One offers a bath, the other a walk-in rainfall shower.

Kitchen

Fully equipped

A proper kitchen with induction hob, built-in oven and microwave, a Dolce Gusto coffee machine and a breakfast bar. A professional knife set, full cookware and cooking essentials are provided, along with a welcome selection of tea, coffee and biscuits.
