The apartment

Two minutes to four tube lines

Step out onto Edgware Road and London's energy is immediate: cafés, late-night eateries and one of the city's best Middle Eastern dining strips. Step back inside and the apartment is calm and stylish, with natural light, blackout blinds and a dedicated workspace.

This is one of two apartments we run in the same period building on Edgware Road — the other, Miles Edgware 2, sleeps four more, so the two together suit a group of up to nine travelling as one party. This apartment has its own entrance and keys. Please note there is no lift in the building.

There is no lift in the building, so access is via stairs.
