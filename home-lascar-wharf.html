The apartment

Riverside living with far-reaching views

Set on the sixth floor, this generous apartment opens onto a private wraparound balcony overlooking Canary Wharf and the surrounding Docklands. At approximately 100 square metres, it offers a bright open-plan living and dining space, a fully equipped kitchen, two comfortable bedrooms and a sofa bed for additional guests.

Limehouse DLR is a short walk away, placing Canary Wharf, Bank and Stratford within easy reach. The Thames Path and Regent's Canal are also nearby, offering a quieter way to explore this characterful part of East London.

Living & dining

Open plan · sofa bed

A bright open-plan living and dining space, filled with natural light from the wraparound glazing. A comfortable sofa, Smart TV and fast Wi-Fi make the apartment equally suited to unwinding or working from home.

The balcony

City and canal views

The private wraparound balcony offers far-reaching views towards Canary Wharf, the canal and surrounding rooftops, with space to enjoy breakfast outside or unwind at the end of the day.

Bedrooms

Two queens

Two well-appointed bedrooms, each with a queen-size bed, quality bedding and generous storage. The second bedroom features a statement wall and bay window with views across the surrounding cityscape.

Kitchen

Fully equipped

A fully equipped kitchen with an oven, hob and washing machine, alongside cookware, crockery and glassware. The adjoining dining area is ideal for everything from relaxed breakfasts to evenings spent dining at home.

Bathroom

Modern · walk-in shower

A modern bathroom with a walk-in shower, stocked with shampoo and body wash. Fresh towels and linen are provided for every stay, with a hairdryer and iron to hand.

The building & waterside

Limehouse Basin

The building sits directly on Limehouse Basin, with the marina outside and the Thames Path and Regent's Canal on the doorstep.
